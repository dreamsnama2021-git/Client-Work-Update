Which slot layout should each Static / Reel entry use?

Both options below track the same thing — Slot 1 (4 done) and Slot 2 (2 done), adding up to 6 of 8 target, with a per-slot Client/Team approval toggle. They only differ in how much date detail sits on each slot card.

Option A

One date per slot

Simpler

Static

target 8
Slot 1 4/8
Posted 5 Sep, 2:00 PM
Approval Client Team
Slot 2 2/8
Posted 7 Sep, 1:00 PM
Approval Client Team
+ Add Slot
Total Complete / Target 6/8

One field: "Posted". Fast to fill in — you log a slot the moment it goes out. No history of how the approval moved, just where it landed right now.

Option B

Three dates per slot

Full timeline

Static

target 8
Slot 1 4/8
Ready 5 Sep, 2:00 PM
Sent to client 5 Sep, 3:00 PM
Approved 7 Sep, 2:00 PM
Approval Client Team
Slot 2 2/8
Ready 7 Sep, 1:00 PM
Sent to client 7 Sep, 3:00 PM
Approved 10 Sep, 2:00 PM
Approval Client Team
+ Add Slot
Total Complete / Target 6/8

Three fields: Ready, Sent to client, Approved. Shows the full turnaround per slot (here: 2 days from ready to approved) — useful for spotting slow approvals, but three date pickers to fill in every time.
